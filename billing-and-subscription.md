# Billing & Subscription (semantic.cv)

_Last updated: 2026‑04‑17_

This page explains how billing, subscriptions, and Pro features work on semantic.cv.

---

## 1. Subscription Overview
semantic.cv offers an optional subscription (“Pro”) that unlocks additional features.  
Your public CV and free themes remain available without a subscription.

A subscription is not required to:

- host your CV  
- use free themes  
- install the GitHub App  
- update your `cv.json`  

A subscription is only required for Pro‑specific functionality.

---

## 2. Billing and Renewal
Subscriptions are billed through **Stripe** and renew automatically at the interval shown during checkout.

- You will be charged at the start of each billing period  
- Renewal continues until you cancel  
- You can cancel at any time  

semantic.cv does **not** store payment card details.  
All payment information is handled securely by Stripe.

---

## 3. Cancelling Your Subscription
You may cancel your subscription at any time.

After cancellation:

- You keep access to Pro features until the end of the current billing period  
- Your CV remains hosted  
- You retain access to all free features  
- Pro‑only features will be disabled after the billing period ends  

Canceling your subscription does **not** delete your data or uninstall the GitHub App.

---

## 4. What Happens After Cancellation
When your subscription ends:

- Your Stripe customer record remains stored as required for accounting and tax compliance  
- Your hosted CV remains available  
- You may re‑subscribe at any time  

No CV data is removed unless you remove or rename `cv.json` or uninstall the GitHub App.

---

## 5. Refunds
Refunds are handled according to the [Refund Policy](./refund-policy.md).

Refunds may be issued in cases such as:

- duplicate charges  
- billing errors  
- technical issues preventing access to Pro features  

Refunds are evaluated case‑by‑case and issued through Stripe.

---
