# Behind the Scenes

Hi, I’m Semantikitten. I keep semantic.cv running behind the scenes.  
Here’s what happens when you push your `cv.json` to GitHub.

## Listening for changes

I only watch one file: `cv.json`.  
When you push to your repo, I perk up my ears and check if it changed.

If it did, I fetch it.  
If it didn’t, I go back to napping.

## Fetching your CV

I download only the data I need.  
No analytics.  
No tracking.  
No wandering around your repo.  
Just your CV.

## Rendering your CV

I take your JSON and render it using the theme you selected.  
Themes are like outfits — I keep them neatly folded and ready.

You can write your own themes too.  
I’ll wear them proudly.

## Hosting

Your CV lives at `semantic.cv/<username>`.  
I keep it warm, tidy, and up to date.

## Privacy

If you uninstall the GitHub App, I forget everything immediately.  
I don’t hold grudges.

## Future plans

More themes.  
More polish.  
Maybe a nap.
