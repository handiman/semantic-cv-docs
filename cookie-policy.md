# Cookie Policy (semantic.cv)

_Last updated: 2026‑04‑17_

semantic.cv uses a minimal set of cookies required for the service to function.

---

## 1. Authentication Cookies
If you use features that require authentication (e.g., signing in with GitHub), semantic.cv sets a session cookie to keep you logged in.

This cookie:
- contains no personal data  
- is used only for authentication  
- is removed when you sign out or when the session expires  

Authentication is optional and not required to host a CV.

---

## 2. No Tracking
semantic.cv does **not** use:
- analytics cookies  
- advertising cookies  
- third‑party tracking cookies  
- cross‑site tracking technologies  

We do not use cookies for profiling or marketing.

---

## 3. Managing Cookies
You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.  
Removing authentication cookies will sign you out of any authenticated features.
