# Cookie Policy (semantic.cv)

_Last updated: 2026‑04‑08_

semantic.cv uses a minimal set of cookies required for the service to function.

## 1. Authentication Cookies
When you sign in with GitHub, we set a session cookie to keep you logged in.  
This cookie contains no personal data and is removed when you sign out.

## 2. No Tracking
semantic.cv does not use:
- analytics cookies  
- advertising cookies  
- third‑party tracking  

## 3. Managing Cookies
You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.  
Removing authentication cookies will sign you out.
