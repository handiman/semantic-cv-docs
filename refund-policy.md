# Refund Policy (semantic.cv)
_Last updated: 2026‑04‑17_

This policy explains how refunds are handled for subscriptions and paid features on semantic.cv.

---

## 1. Subscriptions
semantic.cv offers subscription‑based access to certain features.  
Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled.

You may cancel your subscription at any time.  
After cancellation, you will retain access to free features and hosting of your CV, but lose access to subscription‑only features at the end of the current billing period.

---

## 2. Refunds
Refunds are generally **not provided** for:

- partial billing periods  
- unused time  
- forgetting to cancel before renewal  
- loss of access due to changes in your GitHub repository or configuration  

However, we may issue a refund in cases of:

- accidental duplicate charges  
- technical issues that prevent access to subscription features  
- billing errors  
- other situations where required by applicable law  

Refunds are evaluated on a case‑by‑case basis.

---

## 3. How to Request a Refund
To request a refund, contact:

**billing@semantic.cv**

Include:

- the email associated with your Stripe payment  
- the approximate date of the charge  
- a brief description of the issue  

We may ask for additional information to verify the request.

---

## 4. Payment Processing
All payments are processed securely by Stripe.  
semantic.cv does not store payment card details.

Refunds, when approved, are issued through Stripe to the original payment method.

---

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this refund policy from time to time.  
Continued use of the service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
